Graphic Design

In my digital creators course I took, we had to design for a nonprofit. My nonprofit was aina community initatives. I designed it with kalo and puakenikeni flowers. We then took our design and did mockups like tote bags, notebooks, and many more. This was a long but fast process that I went through from picking the colors, fonts, and much more.

After 12 weeks of hard work I earned my Digital Creators certificate. We learned about graphic design, digital marketing, and storytelling. This was a course offered to Native Hawaiians. I had to submit my resume and a video answering questions in order to eventually get accepted. I was very proud of getting in and finishing all the way through and getting my certificate!
